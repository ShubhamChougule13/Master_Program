Science and Mathematical Expressions

Water: H2O
H2O is the chemical formula for water.

Carbon Dioxide: CO2
CO2 is a gas produced during respiration and combustion.

Oxygen: O2
O2 is essential for human and animal respiration.

Square: x2
x2 means x multiplied by x.

Cube: x3
x3 means x multiplied by x three times.

Einstein's Equation: E = mc2
E = mc2 represents the relationship between energy, mass and the speed of light.
